Photograph plants, animals and fungi, get instant identification suggestions, and share your sightings with a worldwide community of naturalists.

This is an independent build of the open-source iNaturalist app with extra tools for active observers. It works with your existing iNaturalist account.

WHAT'S NEW
• Identify view: review observations one at a time, swipe through their photos, and agree or move on with a tap.
• Multi-species search: search Explore for several taxa at once, and exclude the ones you don't want.
• Hotspots: enter a route and see clusters of observations along it, with the species found at each stop.
• Cropper and brightness editor: crop each photo to its subject and brighten or darken it before you get suggestions.

EVERYTHING ELSE YOU EXPECT
• Identification suggestions from an on-device model, even offline
• Record sounds and add multiple photos to an observation
• Explore observations by place, species, person or date
• Keep track of every species you have seen
